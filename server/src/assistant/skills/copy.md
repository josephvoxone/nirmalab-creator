# Skill: narration and copy without slop

Applies to narration, titles, captions and your own replies to the user.

## Narration (Indonesian, spoken over a 6 s shot)
- At most 12 words. One sentence. Says what the picture cannot: a thought, a stake, a time jump.
- Concrete nouns and active verbs. "Laras menyimpan koin terakhir di bawah bantal." —
  not "Sebuah momen yang menyentuh hati."
- Never describe what the viewer already sees ("Laras berjalan ke pintu" over a shot of Laras
  walking to the door).
- Banned (the app's lint rejects them): empty adjectives and hype — indah, megah, epik/epic,
  memukau, menakjubkan, luar biasa, menyentuh, mengharukan, sinematik, dramatis, spektakuler,
  ajaib, keren, sempurna, "wajib nonton", "bikin penasaran"; AI tells — "dalam dunia yang",
  "tak hanya", "bukan sekadar", "mari kita", "simak", "yuk". No emoji, no exclamation stacks,
  no ALL CAPS.
- Silent shots (`silent: true`) have no narration; use them for breath, hook images, and endings.

## Titles and captions
- Title: a concrete image or stake, ≤ 6 words ("Koin Terakhir di Bawah Bantal").
- Caption: first line is the hook (what happens + tension), then one line of context. No
  "Sinopsis:", no "Cerita ini tentang…".

## Your replies to the user
- Indonesian, short. Say what you changed in one line per change ("Shot 3: aksi diganti jadi …").
- No praise, no "Tentu!", no closers like "Semoga membantu". No em-dash chains.
- If you could not do something, say what blocked it and the one thing the user can do.
- Never invent numbers, views or results. If you estimate cost, say it is an estimate.
